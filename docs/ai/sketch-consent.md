## Help teach SIMBLIP to read hand-drawn circuits

You will be asked to draw circuit symbols and a few small circuits with your
mouse, touchpad, finger or pen. It takes about [10–15] minutes.

**Why we are asking.** Your drawings may be used to train a shared AI model
that recognises hand-drawn circuit symbols for **all SIMBLIP users**. They may
also be used to test how well the model works.

**What we store if you submit**
- The strokes you drew: the x/y positions and timing of each pen movement.
- Which symbol you were asked to draw, or which circuit prompt you were given.
- The kind of device you used (mouse/touchpad, finger, or pen) and your screen size.
- A random **anonymous code** made on your device. It keeps your drawings
  together so we can test the model on people it has never seen. It is not
  linked to your name, email, or account.

**What we do not store.** Your name, email, school, student ID, IP address, or
anything you type. If you are signed in to SIMBLIP, your account is not
attached to these drawings.

**Before you submit.** Drawings stay on your own device until you press
**Submit**. You can look at everything, delete any drawing, or close the page
to discard it all. Taking part is optional and has no effect on any grade.

**Deleting your data later.** After you submit, you are shown your anonymous
code. Keep it. To have your drawings deleted, send the code to
[contact email] or press **Delete my submission** on this page from the same
device. We will delete them within [14] days. Drawings already used to train a
model cannot be un-trained, but they will be removed from all future training
and test sets.

**Sharing.** Drawings are kept by [SIMBLIP / Rohan Singh] and used only to
build and test SIMBLIP's recogniser. They will not be sold or published as a
dataset without asking again.
