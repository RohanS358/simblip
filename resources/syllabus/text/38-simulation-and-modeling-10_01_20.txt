Course Objectives:
The objective of this course is to develop knowledge of modeling and simulation
techniques for discrete and continuous systems. It emphasizes the development and
analysis of simulation models, generation and testing of random numbers and variables,
and application of simulation methods to evaluate the performance of stochastic systems.
