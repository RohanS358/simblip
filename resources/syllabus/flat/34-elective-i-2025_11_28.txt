and computational models of quantum computing. It emphasizes on algorithmic
insights, and offer practical experience with quantum programming environments. By
the end of the course, students will be able to represent and manipulate quantum
states and gates, design and simulate basic quantum circuits, implement fundamental
quantum algorithms and their speedup mechanisms, and explore applications of
quantum machine learning (QML) techniques.
