Course Objectives:
The objective of this course is to impart fundamental knowledge on the design and
implementation of data structures for storing information. It also covers various
algorithms used in computer science. Upon completion of this course, students will be
able to design and choose the appropriate data structure and efficient algorithm to
achieve optimal performance
